Welcome to Burnos
Your next idea. Already in context.
https://burnos.app

1. Launch Burnos from the Start menu.
2. Leave it open while it downloads the voice models. The orb shows progress.
   This first launch needs an Internet connection and approximately 1 GB of downloads.
3. When Settings opens, enter your own OpenRouter API key and choose your models.
   OpenRouter usage may incur charges on your account.
4. Start talking. Mnemosyne memory starts automatically with Burnos.

No Python, Qt development kit, compiler, or separate memory setup is needed.
Click the orb to mute/unmute. Double-click to stop. Right-click for Settings.
While muted, local command recognition can remain active.

Your memory is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BurnOS\memory.
New voice model downloads are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BurnOS\models.
A complete older model installation in %ProgramData%\BurnOS is reused.
Your OpenRouter key is encrypted for your Windows user with DPAPI.
The uninstaller removes the application but keeps your memory, models, and settings.

Conversation and selected memory context go to your configured model provider.
With an OpenRouter key, memory embeddings also use OpenRouter.
Optional Composio connections need your own configuration.
Browser research uses Microsoft Edge, Chrome, or Brave already installed on your PC.

This release is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or SmartScreen warning.
